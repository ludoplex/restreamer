## KEY FINDINGS

### School Choice Overlap
- **17 of 18 Universal ESA states** have overlap with at least one industrial category
- **Texas leads** with 8/8 category overlap AND highest ESA funding ($10K)
- ESA funding ranges from $4,600 (WV) to $10,000 (TX)

### Data Center Concentration
- **Virginia** remains #1 for total capacity but limited school choice
- **Texas, Ohio, Georgia** offer both school choice AND major DC development
- **Louisiana** emerging with Meta Hyperion (potentially largest in US)

### Critical Minerals
- **California** has only active REE mine (Mountain Pass)
- **Texas** has only US REE magnet production (Fort Worth)
- **Nevada** has only active lithium mine (Silver Peak) + Thacker Pass development
- **Arkansas** has largest untapped lithium (Smackover Formation - 19M MT)

### SMR/Nuclear Leadership
- **Tennessee** will host FIRST commercial SMR in US (Clinch River)
- **Michigan** will host Holtec Palisades Pioneer reactors
- **Utah** planning up to 10 SMRs
