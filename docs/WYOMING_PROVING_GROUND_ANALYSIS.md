## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Wyoming has been selected as the **proving ground location** for Mighty House Inc.'s "Computer Store" concept. Success here over the next four years will validate the model before expansion to other strategic locations (Texas, Arizona, Ohio, Georgia, Utah, Tennessee).

| Factor | Wyoming Advantage |
|--------|-------------------|

Wyoming is the ideal proving ground because:

1. **Highest overlap score (8/8)** when properly analyzed
2. **#1 business tax climate** minimizes operating costs
3. **Universal school choice ($7K ESA)** provides student funding
